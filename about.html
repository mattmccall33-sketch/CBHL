What is ball hockey?

Ball hockey is hockey played on foot. Instead of skates and a puck, players wear running shoes and play with a ball on a rink surface. You get the passing, shooting and teamwork of hockey without needing to skate.

It’s fast, easy to pick up, and a great workout. League games follow the CBHL rule book, which is based on the USA Ball Hockey rules.

Where we play

All games are played outdoors at the Thomas Knox rink in Westerville.

We run at least three sessions a year: spring, summer and fall. Check the schedule for game nights and times.

What we play for

Every session, teams compete for the Fattey’s Cup.

We also recognize individual award winners each session:

  • Most Valuable Player
  • Top Forward
  • Top Defense
  • Top Goalie

See every champion on the Past Winners page.

Our NBHL teams

The CBHL also sends two teams to compete in the National Ball Hockey League:

  • The Counts, Tier 2
  • The Dukes, Tier 3
