Join TheCBHL group

Free on iPhone and Android

The league sends game-day updates through a WhatsApp group called TheCBHL. If a game is moved or rained out, that’s where you’ll hear about it first.

Group nameTheCBHL

WhatsApp groups can’t be found by searching. A league admin adds you, so follow the steps below.

1. Get WhatsApp

Skip this if you already have it

iPhone

Open the App Store, search for WhatsApp Messenger, and tap Get.

Open in the App Store

Android

Open the Google Play Store, search for WhatsApp Messenger, and tap Install.

Open in Google Play

Make sure the app is from WhatsApp LLC. WhatsApp is free; it uses Wi‑Fi or your phone’s data, not your text plan. Desktop and web versions are on whatsapp.com/download.

2. Set it up

About two minutes

  1. Open WhatsApp and tap Agree and continue.
  2. Enter your mobile number. WhatsApp texts you a 6-digit code. Type it in to confirm the number is yours.
  3. Add your name and, if you like, a photo. Use the name your teammates know you by so they can tell who’s messaging.
  4. Allow notifications when your phone asks, so you don’t miss a rain-out.

3. Join TheCBHL

A league admin adds you

Email the league with the mobile number you used for WhatsApp. We’ll add you to TheCBHL, and the group will appear at the top of your Chats list.

Email to join the group

Already know someone in the group? If the group settings allow it, they can add you too.

Using the group

The basics

Read and reply

  • Tap Chats, then TheCBHL, to see every message.
  • Type in the box at the bottom and tap the send arrow to reply to everyone.
  • To reply to one message, swipe it to the right (or press and hold it, then tap Reply).

Notifications

  • Keep notifications on for TheCBHL. Game changes are posted here first.
  • If it gets busy, open the group, tap its name at the top, and choose Notifications to adjust them. Muting means you won’t get alerts, so check the group before game nights.

Group etiquette

  • Keep it about the league: games, subs, scores and rides.
  • Need a sub? Post the game date and time.
  • Be respectful. The same code of conduct applies here as on the rink.

Leaving the group

  • Open TheCBHL, tap the group name at the top, scroll to the bottom and tap Exit group.
  • Want back in later? Just email the league again.

Need help?

We’re happy to walk you through it

Ask anyone at the rink on game night, or email the league. WhatsApp also has its own help pages at faq.whatsapp.com.
